Call the Skill tool with **grilling**.

Type `grill-me` when you want your thinking stress-tested before you commit to a plan, decision, or design. The agent interviews you in rounds: each round asks several questions with a recommended answer for each, and you answer the whole batch before the next round begins. Expect roughly 3–6 rounds for a plan of typical size.

To get the most out of it:

- **Answer every question in the round**, even with a "not that" or "deal." An unanswered question stalls the frontier and therefore you.
- **Push back on any recommendation you disagree with** rather than silently accepting it — overriding the default is how the tree gets sharper.
- **Bring the real constraints early** (deadlines, budget, stakeholders). Constraints steer the questions before they're ever asked.
- **Reserve the session for a single plan.** If the topic splits into two viable plans, say so and offer to grill them one at a time.
